# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the problem, what forces are in play, what constraints shape the answer? One or two paragraphs. Link to the Wen365 `CONTEXT.md` terms where relevant.

## Decision

What we will do. Imperative, present tense: "We use X", not "We will use X" or "We chose X". One paragraph.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what is now ruled out. Include both positive and negative. Bullet points fine.

---

_This file follows [Michael Nygard's ADR format](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions). Copy this template to `NNNN-slug.md` (increment the highest existing number) when writing a new ADR. Keep them terse — ADRs capture **that** a decision was made and **why**, not long-form design._
